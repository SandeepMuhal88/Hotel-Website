# Las Cabanas Resort Pushkar 🌿

**Live Demo:** [las-cabanas-resort-kappa.vercel.app](https://las-cabanas-resort-kappa.vercel.app/)

## Introduction

Las Cabanas Resort Pushkar is a modern, responsive website built for a boutique resort located in Ganahera, Pushkar — just 3.2 km from the sacred Pushkar Lake. The site showcases the resort's private garden cottages, swimming pool, free organic breakfast, and authentic Rajasthani hospitality, giving visitors an immersive digital preview of their stay before booking.

The project focuses on clean UI/UX, fast load times, and SEO-friendly structure (meta tags, Open Graph, and Twitter card support) to help the resort attract direct bookings and rank better in search results — reducing dependency on third-party booking platforms.

### Key Highlights
- 🏡 Elegant showcase of garden cottages and amenities
- 🏊 Swimming pool & pet-friendly stay information
- 🍳 Free organic breakfast highlighted for guests
- 📍 Location details — 3.2 km from Pushkar Sacred Lake
- ⚡ Deployed on Vercel for fast, reliable performance
- 🔍 SEO-optimized with proper meta descriptions & social share previews
